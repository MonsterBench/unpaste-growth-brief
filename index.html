Assistagram · Growth brief for Unpaste

Unpaste’s next growth move

A short, plain-English read (about 5 minutes) on how creator videos, a clear offer, and a profit check could help Unpaste find new customers.

Start reading ↓

Prepared by Assistagram · Based on public information as of October 2, 2026 · Not a financial audit

The short version

Top takeaway

Use real creators to show the product, send shoppers to a clear offer, and test whether new customers are profitable.

Opportunity

Unpaste is easy to find, but hard to “get” in a scroll.

Public pages show a real brand with its own store, Amazon, and retail and wholesale listings. The two Meta ads we could see in the snapshot don’t yet tell a first-time shopper what the tablets are like to use.

Recommended approach

Creators on Meta, run through Trybe.

Real people demonstrate the product in short videos, shown as ads on Facebook and Instagram, and lead to a page that matches the video. Trybe is the tool we recommend for running this; Meta does not require it.

Confirm first

The numbers behind the business.

We can’t see Unpaste’s private spend, revenue, or profit. Before spending more on ads, we need to confirm what an order really earns, and that creators’ content can be used in ads.

The plan in three steps

In priority order. Each step makes the next one work better.

  1. Make the offer and buying page clear

    Pick one easy-to-understand offer for new customers and send ad clicks to a page built for it. In the October 2 snapshot, the Meta ads we saw led to the general homepage or to individual product pages.

  2. Launch a Meta + Trybe creator program

    Find creators with different, believable reasons to use Unpaste, get written permission for how their videos are used, and run the best ones as Facebook and Instagram ads. The existing No Tube Club can keep doing what it does well; this complements it.

  3. Measure profit before scaling

    Track what it costs to win a new customer and what is left from their first order. Spend more only if the numbers hold up.

Plain-English terms

Meta
The company behind Facebook and Instagram, and the ads shown on them.
Trybe
A service for finding creators, collecting their content, and setting up partnership ads. It is part of our recommended program, not something Meta requires.
Partnership ad
An ad that runs from a creator’s own account, with their permission, so it looks like a recommendation from a real person.
Customer acquisition cost (CAC)
How much it costs, in total, to win one new customer.
Contribution margin
What is left from an order after the direct costs of that sale (product, shipping, fees, ads). Simply: is the first order profitable?

How it would work

One simple path from a creator’s video to a repeat customer.

  1. 1Creator videoA real person shows the product
  2. 2Facebook / Instagram adThe video runs as a partnership ad
  3. 3Matched product / offer pageThe page repeats what the video promised
  4. 4PurchaseA clear, easy checkout
  5. 5Repeat purchaseFollow-up that fits what they bought

Where Trybe fits: it helps with steps 1 and 2 (finding creators, collecting content and permissions, and launching partnership ads). Results depend on the offer, the product, and the numbers, and we can’t guarantee any outcome.

Existing No Tube Club: Unpaste already rewards fans for sharing. That’s complementary. A reward or a public post doesn’t automatically give permission to use someone’s content in paid ads, so that is checked separately.

Creative ideas to test

Different reasons to care, rather than many versions of the same video.

1

Travel without the tube

Packing and using tablets on the go.

2

Chew, brush, foam

A real-time first-use demo that answers “how does this work?” honestly.

3

Everyday low-waste

Show the routine itself, instead of broad environmental claims.

4

What it really feels like

Texture, foam, and the new formula, with no claim that everyone loves it.

5

Founder story

The origin story, paired with verified current product facts.

6

Qualified educator

Approved, factual oral-care education without unsupported health claims.

A three-phase pilot

A proposed order of work to discuss. It is not an approved scope, budget, timeline, or promise of results.

Phase 1

Prepare

Confirm margins, inventory, product claims, and creator permissions. Choose one offer and a matched page. Make sure purchases are tracked from ad to checkout.

Phase 2

Test

Run a few genuinely different creator concepts as Meta ads, using a creator’s account only with their permission. Compare results under similar conditions.

Phase 3

Decide

Look at cost per new customer and first-order profit, plus whether people come back. If the numbers work, expand. If not, find out why before spending more.

A note on reading results: low ad delivery doesn’t always mean weak creative, and how long an ad has been running doesn’t prove it performs. Views and clicks alone don’t show whether an ad actually brought in profitable customers.

Five questions to start the conversation

  1. How much revenue comes from your own website, Amazon, and wholesale, and which channel do you most want to grow?
  2. What are you spending on Meta and Google now, and what has actually worked for new-customer acquisition?
  3. What contribution remains on the best-selling order after product, fulfillment, shipping, and fees?
  4. How often do buyers reorder, and how has the new formula changed repeat purchase and subscriptions?
  5. What has No Tube Club produced, what paid-use rights do you hold, and do you already have a TikTok Shop seller account?

What this brief can and can’t tell you

  • It uses only public information, from an original research snapshot dated October 2, 2026. Prices, counts, ads, and pages may have changed since; nothing here is a live check.
  • We have no access to private spend, revenue, or profit. This brief contains no revenue estimates.
  • Creator rights and product claims need verification before anything is published. “FDA registered” is not “FDA approved.”
  • TikTok Shop was not verified: that means not established, not proven absent.
  • This is not a financial audit, and it is not an endorsement or approved scope.

Research details & sources

The verified facts behind the plan. Expand any item. Everything is a point-in-time snapshot as of October 2, 2026, from public pages.

Channels, products and prices

Where Unpaste sells

  • Own store: brand site at unpaste.us (Wix) leads to store.unpaste.us (Shopify).
  • Amazon: a 125-count listing sold by Unpaste and shipped by Amazon, showing 4.5 stars from 203 ratings and Subscribe & Save. Ratings may combine variants and don’t show sales volume.
  • Wholesale and retail: an official wholesale portal and retailer directory, plus third-party listings (Dental Lace, Garage Grown Gear, Zero Waste Outlet, EarthHero, Grove, ZeroWasteStore). Amazon’s founder feature describes earlier wholesale-only roots. Which channel earns the most is unknown.

Observed prices

  • Fluoride Mint 125 count: $12.99; 250 count: $21.99; mixed 125-count bundle: $24; fluoride-free 125 count: $12.99.
  • Fluoride subscriptions showed 15% off (calculated $11.04 for 125, $18.69 for 250), every two to six months.
  • Free continental-US shipping was advertised on orders over $30. Each single listing above sits below that. Two small packs ($25.98), three ($38.97) and two large packs ($43.98) are arithmetic examples, not existing named offers.
  • The FAQ says orders ship from Arizona in one to two business days. Checkout shipping, fees below the threshold, inventory, and wholesale economics were not tested.

Social and reviews

  • Instagram: 3,242 followers; Facebook: 309; both active. Counts don’t show engagement or customer quality.
  • Visible Shopify reviews were fewer than Amazon’s: 250-count 4.6 (14), 125-count fluoride 4.0 (3), mixed bundle none. Check reuse rights, and don’t imply Amazon reviews apply to a specific item.

Avespan, LLC owns the Unpaste trademark; founders Lin Adler and Patrick Graham are useful context, not proof of current operations.

Website friction we noticed
  • Broken retailer links: in the retailer directory, the labels for Garage Grown Gear, Zero Waste Outlet, and EarthHero all pointed to ZeroWasteStore. The links should go to the intended stores.
  • No dedicated landing page seen: the $24 mixed bundle was reachable from an ad and the store but wasn’t among the three products on the inspected /shop page. Private or unindexed pages may exist.
  • Path to purchase: ads and social lead to the Wix brand site, then a shop selector, a Shopify product page, and checkout. Checkout was not tested.
  • Older descriptions: some articles and retailers describe older formula details. A current, product-specific content and claims pack would help.
  • Reorder fit: travel-only buyers may not become daily subscribers. Measure reorders by use case before treating 15% subscriptions as predictable.

Claims: don’t treat fluoride-fear, safe-to-swallow, detox, child-safety, enamel, or cavity claims as established. Get product-specific substantiation and qualified review before publishing.

Creators, affiliates and TikTok: what is uncertain
  • No Tube Club: the It’s Layered program rewards sharing. A public post offered $6.50 toward the next order for a pouch-by-sink photo. This is not an established sales-commission affiliate program, and a reward or post doesn’t grant paid-ad or editing rights.
  • Comment-for-link: an October 2025 creator reel asks viewers to comment “UNPASTE” for a link by DM. Current automation, gifting, and compensation were not verified.
  • TikTok Shop: not verified. No official account, seller storefront, listing, or affiliate terms were confirmed. TikTok access was limited by login and location, so this is not established, not proven absent. Shopify’s Shop app is not TikTok Shop.
  • Third-party discussion exists, such as an indexed creator TikTok and a Garage Grown Gear video; their commercial relationships were not verified. Retailer affiliate links don’t prove a direct Unpaste affiliate deal.
  • Trybe: describes itself as offering Shopify sync, creator submissions, sample requests, compensation options, Meta partnership-ad launches, and server-side last-click attribution. These are vendor claims, not verified in an Unpaste account, and Trybe is not TikTok Shop.
Traffic and measurement
  • Google ads: US Ads Transparency searches for unpaste.us and Unpaste returned no matches. The Shopify page loaded Google Analytics and Google Ads tags (including AW-605121744). A search miss doesn’t prove no ads, and a tag doesn’t prove current spend.
  • Meta pixel: the same pixel ID, 1154155735717780, loaded on Wix and Shopify. Purchase accuracy, consent, server-side events, and de-duplication are unverified.
  • Traffic estimates: Similarweb was blocked, so no defensible visit or channel numbers were recovered.
  • Editorial: Wirecutter, Tilted Map, and retailer content may be discovery surfaces, but they are not measured traffic or attributed sales.
