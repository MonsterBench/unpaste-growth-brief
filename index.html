01 / Executive view

A credible test. A measured next step.

Unpaste has real distribution and repeat-purchase infrastructure. The visible acquisition system has useful pieces, but public evidence does not establish readiness for aggressive paid scaling.

Recommended

Our recommended Meta program is built around Trybe: rights-cleared creator work, creator-handle ads, a stronger buying path, and measured repeat purchase.

Start with a bounded test. Scale only when first-order contribution and payback support it. Trybe is central to this proposed program, while the existing No Tube Club can continue serving community and advocacy.

The decision in one line

Test the system before buying more reach.

Margins, creator rights, claims, tracking, inventory, and offer fit are gates. A creator platform cannot answer those questions on its own.

02 / What is visible

A business with several working surfaces.

Public storefronts show a brand with distribution and a current formula. They do not show which channel generates the most revenue.

Verified / DTC

Wix to Shopify

Marketing at unpaste.us leads into store.unpaste.us. Fluoride Mint 125 count is $12.99; 250 count is $21.99; the mixed 125-count bundle is $24. A fluoride-free 125 count is also $12.99.

Observed fluoride subscriptions offer 15% off (calculated $11.04 for 125, $18.69 for 250) and two-to-six-month intervals. Advertised free continental-US shipping applies to orders over $30.

Verified / Marketplace

Amazon

A current 125-count listing says sold by Unpaste and shipped by Amazon. Its snapshot showed 4.5 stars / 203 ratings and Subscribe & Save pricing.

Ratings may aggregate variants. They do not establish sales volume or revenue.

Verified / Distribution

Wholesale & retail

An official wholesale portal and retailer directory sit alongside third-party listings, including Dental Lace, Garage Grown Gear, Zero Waste Outlet, EarthHero, Grove, and ZeroWasteStore. Amazon’s founder feature describes earlier wholesale-only roots.

Current store count, sell-through, fulfillment capacity, and the leading revenue channel remain unknown.

Commercial evidence and offer math

The $12.99 single, $21.99 large pack, and $24 mixed bundle each sit below the advertised over-$30 free-shipping threshold. Two small packs cost $25.98 at current prices; three cost $38.97; two large packs cost $43.98. The last two are arithmetic examples, not existing named offers. A household multipack is worth testing only if demand and contribution support it.

The official FAQ says orders ship from Arizona in one to two business days. Checkout shipping enforcement, shipping fees below the threshold, current manufacturer, inventory capacity, and wholesale economics were not tested. Avespan, LLC owns the Unpaste trademark; founders Lin Adler and Patrick Graham are useful context, not proof of current operations.

04 / Opportunity map

Fix the offer, proof, and product story together.

These are hypotheses and observed inconsistencies. They should be validated against actual customer and financial data.

01 / Offer

Design for a household order.

The current hero prices fall below free shipping over $30. Test a convenient multipack or matched offer only if customers want it and contribution survives fulfillment and media costs.

02 / Message

Show the first use.

“New formula” matters to existing customers; a newcomer needs a clear chew-brush-foam demonstration, sensory expectation, practical use case, and proof. Test genuinely different concepts rather than many minor hooks.

03 / Proof

Use reviews precisely.

Inspected Shopify pages had fewer visible reviews than Amazon: 250-count 4.6/14, 125-count fluoride 4.0/3, mixed bundle none. Check reuse rights and avoid suggesting Amazon or variant reviews apply to a specific SKU.

04 / Site

Repair retailer links.

In the live directory, labels for Garage Grown Gear, Zero Waste Outlet, and EarthHero all pointed to ZeroWasteStore. Correct the hrefs so shoppers reach the intended stores.

05 / Content

Update formula descriptions.

Older articles and some retailers describe older formula attributes. Provide a current, SKU-specific content and claims pack; request updates where editorial control allows. Current reactions are mixed, so separate legacy complaints from new-formula feedback.

06 / Retention

Fit the actual usage pattern.

Travel-only buyers may not become daily subscribers. Measure reorder by use case and SKU before treating 15% subscriptions as predictable LTV. First-use guidance and replenishment timing may help.

Claims gate: “FDA registered” is not “FDA approved.” Do not treat fluoride fear, safe-to-swallow, detoxification, child-safety, enamel, or cavity claims as established. Obtain SKU-specific substantiation and qualified review before publishing creative; respect packaging instructions.

Complement the No Tube Club.

Audit members, assets, and results before sourcing more generic content. Keep the community program where it earns advocacy. Use Trybe for incremental rights-cleared performance creative and Meta operations where it fills a demonstrated gap.

Separate every permission.

Organic reposting, brand-handle paid use, editing or derivative use, and creator-handle ads each need their own rights check. A gifted product, a $6.50 reward, or a public post does not supply those rights automatically.

06 / Pilot decision

Earn the scale decision.

A bounded pilot is a proposed sequence, not an approved scope, budget, timeline, deliverable list, or result forecast.

Prepare the ground

Confirm margins, inventory, claims, rights, historical channel data, and the Wix → Shopify → checkout measurement path. Choose one acquisition offer and a matched PDP or lander challenger.

Test the system

Run distinct rights-cleared creator concepts on Meta. Use creator identity only with authorization. Keep catalog and produced creative reporting separate; compare destinations under comparable conditions.

Read contribution

Measure new-customer CAC, net revenue per order, first-order contribution after media and creator costs, subscription uptake, and matured repeat cohorts. Scale only through Finance-approved payback gates.

Decision rule

Expand only when economics clear.

A positive test can support wider creator recruitment and a separately qualified TikTok Shop lane. A weak result calls for diagnosis of offer, product, tracking, or unit economics before buying more creative volume.

Measurement boundary

Keep each cost and sale in its lane.

Separate media, agency, creators and usage rights, software, samples and logistics, and commissions. Keep Amazon and retail context separate from Meta-attributed DTC revenue. CTR, views, ad longevity, and view-through ROAS do not establish incrementality.

Lifecycle and reporting detail

Verify existing email and SMS flows before rebuilding. Candidate improvements are first-use instructions, sensory expectation setting, SKU- and household-specific replenishment, post-reformulation win-back, and clear subscription education.

Use acquisition and cohort reporting to understand new customers, net sales after discounts and returns, first-order contribution after media and creator costs, and repeat contribution when cohorts mature. Do not combine wholesale shipments and consumer retail sales or treat all marketplace revenue as Meta lift.

07 / Conversation guide

Five questions that change the plan.

These answers would turn public-source hypotheses into an informed acquisition decision.

  1. How much revenue comes from your own website, Amazon, and wholesale—and which channel do you most want to grow?
  2. What are you spending on Meta and Google now, and what has actually worked for new-customer acquisition?
  3. What contribution remains on the best-selling order after product, fulfillment, shipping, and fees?
  4. How often do buyers reorder, and how has the new formula changed repeat purchase and subscriptions?
  5. What has No Tube Club produced, what paid-use rights do you hold, and do you already have a TikTok Shop seller account?

Then request read-only Shopify and GA4 data, Meta and Google reporting, Amazon sales and costs, wholesale channel statements, subscription cohorts, inventory, lifecycle flow map, and a creator-rights ledger.
